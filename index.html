Assessment reports

Assessment report

Guide & resources

Trust vs. mistrust

Explore early experiences of safety, consistency, caregiving and trust.

Autonomy vs. shame and doubt

Explore independence, choice, self-control and responses to mistakes.

Initiative vs. guilt

Explore initiative, play, curiosity, responsibility and encouragement.

Industry vs. inferiority

Explore learning, competence, school experiences, feedback and belonging.

Identity vs. role confusion

Explore identity, values, roles, belonging and future aspirations.

Intimacy vs. isolation

Explore closeness, trust, mutuality, friendships and relationships.

Generativity vs. stagnation

Explore contribution, caregiving, work, mentoring and purpose.

Integrity vs. despair

Explore life reflection, meaning, acceptance, regret and legacy.

Practice note: Erikson's stages are broad theoretical themes, not a rigid checklist. People may revisit themes throughout life. Interpret responses alongside the client's current environment, culture, relationships, strengths, trauma history where appropriately disclosed, and their own understanding of events. Seek supervision when risk, safeguarding, mental health or legal issues arise.
